# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, reviews,
and any other project space, is expected to:

* be respectful and considerate, including when disagreeing;
* assume good intent and ask questions before drawing conclusions;
* give and accept constructive technical criticism gracefully;
* focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Not acceptable:

* harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind;
* discriminatory jokes or language;
* publishing other people's private information without permission;
* sustained disruption of discussions;
* using the project to distribute recordings of other people's voices
  without their consent.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project
in public.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer through a private channel listed on the
maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and may temporarily or
permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful, after a warning when the
situation allows one.
